Reply guide

A short set of rules so every reply sounds like the brand, stays accurate, and reaches the right person.

1

Check in

Comments, DMs, and mentions at 10 am and 4 pm every day. Everything gets a reply in the same window.

2

Answer

Sizing, colors, prices, and how to order. Facts come from the shop page only, so nothing gets promised by accident.

3

Pass along

Custom orders, wholesale, and anything I can’t confirm go to the owner. The customer hears what happens next.

4

Escalate

Complaints, order problems, or anything sensitive: apologize publicly, move to DM, and flag it to the owner the same day.

Saved replies

Sizing

Every hat is made to the head measurement you send, in baby, kids, and adult sizes. Wrap a soft tape around the widest part of the head, just above the ears, and enter the number in inches when you order.

Colors

The Egg and Mushroom Hats come in 9 colors: red, blue, green, purple, yellow, orange, black, pink, and white. Just pick one when you order!

Order question

Thanks for reaching out! I’m sending you a DM now so we can look at your order together.

Voice: warm, short, and specific. Use their name, one emoji at most, and never leave a question without a next step.
