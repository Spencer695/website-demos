Feed posts

Each post pairs a product photo with a caption that sells the item, explains sizing, and ends with one clear next step.

Stories

Quick daily touchpoints: one to start a conversation, one to answer the question people ask most, and one to send people to the shop.

Reel plan

A 12 second Reel cut from the same photo shoot. Cuts land on the beat, and on-screen text means it still works with the sound off.

  1. 0:00 to 0:03 · Hook

    On screen: “Which breakfast hat are you?” Slow push in on the Pancake Hat.

  2. 0:03 to 0:06

    On screen: “Sunny side up.” Whip cut to the Egg Hat on the beat.

  3. 0:06 to 0:09 · Twist

    On screen: “Wait, that’s not breakfast.” Jump cut to the Mushroom Hat.

  4. 0:09 to 0:12 · End card

    Logo with “Made to fit. Link in bio.” Fade out.

Caption
Pancake, egg, or mushroom? Comment your pick 👇 Every hat is made to order in baby, kids, and adult sizes.
Audio
Upbeat trending sound, kept low under the text.
Editing
9:16 in CapCut, captions on, text kept out of the bottom fifth so the buttons don’t cover it.
Cover
First frame with the hook text, so it looks good in the profile grid.

How I’d measure it

The goal is shop visits and orders, not just followers. Each week I’d check these in Instagram and Facebook Insights and use them to plan the next week.

Reach and views

Are new people seeing the content? Compare Reels with photo posts each week.

Saves and shares

Stronger signals than likes. They show people want to come back to a post or send it to a friend.

Link taps and DMs

Who tapped through to the shop or asked about sizing, which is the step right before an order.

Orders

Which posts lined up with orders that week, so the next plan does more of what works.
