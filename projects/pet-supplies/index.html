BEFORE YOU STOP BY

A few good answers.

What should I bring to the dog wash?

Bring your dog on a leash and your own brush. Our sample wash setup includes shampoo, conditioner, towels, and a dryer. Details here are illustrative.

Can I book a real appointment here?

This is an independent portfolio concept. Pet Supplies is a fictional business for this project. The booking flow uses sample availability and doesn’t create a real reservation.

Are the products and prices real?

The collection, packaging illustrations, prices, and store details are fictional demo content created to show the shopping experience. No payment is collected.

What’s the idea behind this redesign?

Bring shopping and pet care into one clear neighborhood experience: make services easy to compare, help owners understand the dog wash, and keep booking simple on a phone.
