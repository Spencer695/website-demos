Returns & care

How returns work, how to measure, and how to keep your hat cozy.

Returns

All sales are final. Every hat is handmade to the measurements provided, so returns and exchanges are not accepted.

If an order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact CC's House within 7 days at ccshousecontact@gmail.com with a photo to have it made right.

Caring for your hat

  • Hand wash in cold water.
  • Lay flat or air dry.

How to measure

Every hat comes in baby, kids, and adult sizes and is made to the head measurement you enter when you order.

  1. Grab a soft measuring tape, or a piece of string and a ruler.
  2. Wrap it around the widest part of the head, just above the eyebrows and ears.
  3. Keep it snug but not tight, and write down the number in inches.
