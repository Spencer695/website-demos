How sizing works

Each hat is made to fit. When you order, you'll enter the head measurement in inches, and your hat is made to that size.

Because every hat is made to your measurements, all sales are final. Returns & care

How to measure

  1. Grab a soft measuring tape, or a piece of string and a ruler.
  2. Wrap it around the widest part of the head, just above the eyebrows and ears.
  3. Keep it snug but not tight, and write down the number in inches.
